Shabd Sangam

A fun and challenging Hindi crossword game (वर्ग पहेली). Relive the good old days of waiting for the daily newspaper crossword right on your phone.

Download on Google Play

🧩 Engaging 8x8 Grids

Solve carefully crafted 8x8 crossword puzzles daily. Simply tap an empty box on the grid, then select the correct letter from the pad below to uncover hidden words and complete the shabd paheli.

🧠 Vocabulary Builder

Explore classic and modern Hindi vocabulary. Perfect for students, language learners, and word puzzle fans wanting to exercise their minds and improve focus and memory.

🎨 Clean, Modern UI

Experience a premium, smooth interface built with modern UI effects. Designed with easy drag-and-drop controls for a distraction-free, relaxing gameplay experience across all age groups.

An Authentic Hindi Spelling Experience

Unlike basic word games, Shabd Sangam is designed for true Hindi language lovers. The game features a challenging selection pad of 16-letter tiles to test your spelling. For a genuinely authentic experience, we enforce strict grammatical rules where letters with matras are treated as a single cell, making it the perfect digital recreation of a classic Hindi newspaper puzzle.
