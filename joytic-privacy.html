Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Joytic: Smile Every Day, developed by Winterlight Technologies, handles information when you use the app. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

Information We Collect

Joytic does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

The app stores limited preferences locally on your device, such as app settings and progress, to provide its features.

Camera Usage

Joytic uses your device's front camera to detect smiles as part of the app's core functionality.

  • Camera processing happens entirely on your device.
  • No photos or videos are saved.
  • No camera images are uploaded to our servers.
  • No camera images are shared with any third party.
  • Camera frames are processed only in real time and immediately discarded after smile detection.

Advertising

Joytic displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and other information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Notifications

Joytic may request permission to send optional daily reminder notifications to encourage regular use of the app. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Permissions Used

  • Camera – Used only for real-time smile detection.
  • Notifications – Used for optional daily reminders.

Joytic does not access your contacts, microphone, location, SMS, call logs, or photos.

Third-Party Services

Joytic may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

Joytic does not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Camera data is processed only on your device and is never transmitted to Winterlight Technologies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information used by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Joytic does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided by a child, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with an updated effective date.
